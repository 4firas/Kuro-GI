v2.1, current

Android arm64, game 7.0.0. 1726ff97…e15eb2dd

Safety update. Quieted two client-side telemetry reports the game was packaging into its security traffic — movement-speed and overlay-window. No new switches to flip; the client simply volunteers less now. (How stays private.)

Safe-by-default profile. Fresh installs boot into a locked Profile 1: dangerous server-visible features off, God Mode and No Fall Damage on, Auto Talk / Loot / Open / Waypoint on, ESP on (chests + all oculi, 500 m), stealth teleport defaults. Slots 2–3 are free; loading one is your risk to take.

Teleport points for every region. 21,645 measured points, nothing estimated. Chests, oculi, specialties, waypoints per region:

Plus 956 transmit points (852 waypoints, 41 statues, 63 domains) from the game's own scene data. Locked waypoints get visited on purpose; unlocked ones ride the game's own transmit.

Auto farm with delay slider. Whole selections nearest first, 500 ms to 10 s wait per point, 5 s default, with a random human pause added every hop. Nudge and retry, skip and remember, forced switches restored after.

141 pickable collectibles. Every crop, ore, drop and oculus by name and picture. Picked ones marked and teleportable.

Crash records. Every death writes what, where, and the last minute of breadcrumbs. One button copies it to Downloads for support. No account data in it.

Smoother flight, guarded landings. NoClip writes at frame start with an on-screen stick; long jumps cruise high, descend near the target, and hold the landing while terrain streams in. No void drops, no bounce loops.

ESP overhaul. Ring with picture or glyph, plates, smoothing, fade, off-screen arrows, live counts. On from the first boot.

Menu rework. Teleport and World reorganized top-down, every section says what it is. Closed-menu quick buttons for quest, chest, waypoint, oculus.

Get it from GitHub releases, not re-uploads. Older copies floating around (including the early 2.0) are outdated.
