v2.0 — current

Android arm64, game 7.0.0 · 828b0141…99bf1470

  • Teleport Points — waypoints and statues of every region in one list, exact positions from the game's own scene data. Locked ones are the point: land next to them, auto-press, move on.
  • Auto farm — whole selections nearest-first with a 500 ms – 5 s wait slider, nudge-and-retry, skip-and-remember, forced switches with restore.
  • Offline points by region — chests, oculi, specialties, with approximate pins flagged and handled (arrive high, drop, No Fall on).
  • Items — 141 collectibles by name and picture, picked ones marked and teleportable.
  • ESP overhaul — ring + picture/glyph, plates, smoothing, fade, off-screen arrows, live per-kind counts.
  • Crash records — every death writes what, where, and the last minute of breadcrumbs; one button copies it for support.
  • Safer landings — transmit-first long hauls, guarded arrivals while terrain streams, no more falling through the world.
  • Smooth NoClip — position written at frame start; stick overlay.
  • Menu rework — Teleport and World reorganized top-down, every section says what it is.
