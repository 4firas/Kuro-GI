Safety

Cheating in an online game can get the account banned, up to permanently. Kuro does not hide from the server — it labels, per switch, how exposed you are, and routes movement through the quietest available path. Read this page the way you'd read the dosage label.

NO TOOL IS BAN-PROOF. ALT ACCOUNTS EXIST FOR A REASON.

The pills

PillMeaningExamples
localStays on your device. Display, camera, overlay.FOV, hide damage numbers, ESP visuals, FPS cap, No fall damage.
server-checkableNothing is sent, but the results could look wrong in your data.Speed, NoClip, god mode, damage numbers that don't add up.
sent to serverDirectly visible: unlocks, activations, transmits, picks.Waypoint unlocks, statue activations, map transmit, opening chests.
Unlocking a waypoint through Kuro is the same prompt a walking player presses — the activation itself is normal play. What surrounds it (how you got there, how fast, how many per minute) is what you control.

How movement stays quiet

  • Safe mode (default). Long hauls open with the game's own map transmit — one normal-looking load — then finish in short hops inside the engine's no-packet envelope. The loading screen is the feature working.
  • Direct jumps (safe mode off, or no waypoint near the target) are single displacements the server can see. Fewer, shorter, slower is the entire discipline.
  • Paced mode crawls the distance at a set speed instead of jumping. Slowest, quietest.
  • Reach is capped at 40 m — past that the game stops answering, so Kuro stops asking.
  • After long jumps the landing is guarded while terrain streams in, so you don't fall through the world.

Rules that matter

  • Farm at human hours, human pace. The delay slider exists for a reason; 500 ms across 200 chests at 4 a.m. is a confession.
  • Don't stack red pills. Speed × NoClip × god mode × instant clears, all at once, is how accounts end.
  • Cutscenes and domains: keep movement switches off where the game watches closely.
  • One account per device state. Don't farm on the main and play legit on the same install five minutes later and assume the two don't share a log.
  • New game version? Wait for a Kuro release that names it. Old builds on new clients break in loud ways.

If the worst happens

A ban is a server decision; nothing on your device appeals it. What helps the rest of us: Settings → If something goes wrong → copy the crash record and send it in, so the cause gets fixed instead of repeated. See Help.
