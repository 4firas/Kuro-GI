Safety

Cheating in an online game can end the account, permanently. Kuro doesn't hide from the server. It labels every switch with what it costs and routes movement through the quietest path it has. Read this like a dosage label.

No tool is ban-proof. Alt accounts exist for a reason.

The pills

PillMeaningExamples
localStays on your device. Display, camera, overlay.FOV, hidden damage numbers, ESP visuals, no fall damage. (FPS cap suspended: its target is server-visible.)
server-checkableNothing is sent, but the results could look wrong in your data.Speed, NoClip, god mode, damage that doesn't add up.
sent to serverDirectly visible. Unlocks, activations, transmits, picks.Waypoint unlocks, statue activations, map transmit, opened chests.
Unlocking a waypoint through Kuro is the same prompt a walking player presses, so the activation itself is normal play. What surrounds it is yours to control: how you got there, how fast, how many per minute.

Quiet miles

Safe mode is on by default. Long hauls open with the game's own map transmit, one normal-looking load, then finish in short hops inside the no-packet envelope. That loading screen means it's working. Direct jumps (safe mode off, or no waypoint near) are single displacements the server can see, so fewer, shorter and slower is the whole discipline. Paced mode crawls instead of jumping. Reach stops at 40 m because past that the game stops answering. After long jumps the landing is guarded while terrain streams in.

Rules that matter

Farm at human hours at a human pace. The delay slider exists for a reason, and 500 ms across 200 chests at 4 a.m. reads like a confession. Don't stack red pills: speed times NoClip times god mode times instant clears, all at once, is how accounts end. Keep movement switches off in cutscenes and domains, where the game watches closely. Don't farm on the main and play legit on the same install five minutes later and assume the two don't share a log. New game version means waiting for the Kuro release that names it. Old builds on new clients break loudly.

If the worst happens

A ban is a server decision and nothing on your device appeals it. What helps everyone else: Settings, If something goes wrong, copy the crash record and send it in, so the cause gets fixed instead of repeated. See Help.
