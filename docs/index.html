What it does

Teleport Points

All waypoints and statues per region. Locked ones too.

Auto farm

Whole selections, nearest first, on your delay.

God mode

Damage doesn't land.

No fall damage

Drops don't kill. Use it on approximate pins.

Speed

A multiplier on movement. Keep it sane.

NoClip

Gravity off, velocity flight, on-screen stick.

Why Kuro

Three things it refuses to do.

No silent risk

Every switch carries its pill. Nothing red hides behind a friendly toggle.

No loud miles

Long hauls open with the game's own transmit, then short hops. The loading screen is the feature working.

No mystery crashes

A death writes a record. One button copies it for support.
