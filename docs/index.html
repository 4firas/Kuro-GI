What it does

Short version. The full catalog with details is under Features.

Teleport

Jump to points, marks, picked items, quest target.

Teleport Points

All waypoints and statues per region. Locked ones too.

Auto farm

Whole selections, nearest first, on your delay.

God mode

Damage doesn't land.

No fall damage

Drops don't kill. Use it on approximate pins.

Speed

A multiplier on movement. Keep it sane.

NoClip

Gravity off, velocity flight, on-screen stick.

ESP

Ring, name, distance, off-screen arrows, fade.

Items

141 collectibles by name and picture. Picked ones marked.

Auto loot / open

Presses pickups and prompts for you.

Reach

Prompts appear from 40 m out.

Auto waypoint / statue

Presses unlock prompts on arrival.

Hide damage numbers

Display only. Clean screenshots.

FOV / FPS cap

Camera and frame controls.

Profiles

Save and swap whole setups.

Harvest

Records what you pass. JSON export.

Crash records

What died, where, last minute of breadcrumbs.

Safe mode

Transmit first, short hops after. On by default.
