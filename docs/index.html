Kuro is an in-game overlay for Genshin Impact on Android (arm64): an ESP layer, a teleport network covering every region, an auto-farmer for collectibles, and protection switches — all behind a floating badge, all labeled with exactly how visible each switch is to the server.

What it does

Teleport Points

Every waypoint and statue in every region, by region. Locked ones included — teleport to the position and the game unlocks them on arrival, like a walk-up.

Auto farm

Pick a region and a kind — chests, oculi, specialties, teleport points — and it walks the whole selection nearest-first, collecting through the auto systems, on your delay.

ESP + Items

A ring with a picture or a symbol, name and distance, smoothing and fade. 141 collectibles pickable by name and picture; picked ones are marked and teleportable.

Protection

God mode, no fall damage, movement speed, smooth NoClip flight with an on-screen stick.

Crash records

If the game dies with Kuro on, a record is written: what died, where, and the last minute of breadcrumbs. One button copies it for support.

Safety first

Every switch carries a pill — local server-checkable sent to server — and long teleports ride the game's own transmit plus short, packet-quiet hops.
