Kuro

An overlay for Genshin Impact on Android. ESP, teleports, auto farm. Every switch says what it costs.

Download Quick start Guide

v2.0 · Android arm64 · game 7.0.0 · SHA-256 828b0141…99bf1470

Go anywhere.

Every waypoint and statue in every region, in one list. Locked ones included. You land next to them and the game unlocks them like a walk-up.

teleport > waypoint, Natlan, 320 m
arrived. Auto Waypoint pressed it.

Farm while AFK.

Pick a region and a kind. Kuro walks the whole selection nearest first, collects through the auto systems, waits your delay, remembers what's done.

farm > farming chests, 12 done, 3 skipped
switches forced on for the run, restored after.

Know what each switch costs.

Local stays on your device. Server-checkable could show in your data. Sent to server is directly visible. The pill sits right on the switch.

local FOV, damage numbers, ESP
server-checkable speed, noclip, god mode
sent to server unlocks, transmits, picks

What it does

Short version. The full catalog with details is under Features.

Teleport

Jump to points, marks, picked items, quest target.

Teleport Points

All waypoints and statues per region. Locked ones too.

Auto farm

Whole selections, nearest first, on your delay.

God mode

Damage doesn't land.

No fall damage

Drops don't kill. Use it on approximate pins.

Speed

A multiplier on movement. Keep it sane.

NoClip

Gravity off, velocity flight, on-screen stick.

ESP

Ring, name, distance, off-screen arrows, fade.

Items

141 collectibles by name and picture. Picked ones marked.

Auto loot / open

Presses pickups and prompts for you.

Reach

Prompts appear from 40 m out.

Auto waypoint / statue

Presses unlock prompts on arrival.

Hide damage numbers

Display only. Clean screenshots.

FOV / FPS cap

Camera and frame controls.

Profiles

Save and swap whole setups.

Harvest

Records what you pass. JSON export.

Crash records

What died, where, last minute of breadcrumbs.

Safe mode

Transmit first, short hops after. On by default.

Why Kuro

Three things it refuses to do.

No silent risk

Every switch carries its pill. Nothing red hides behind a friendly toggle.

No loud miles

Long hauls open with the game's own transmit, then short hops. The loading screen is the feature working.

No mystery crashes

A death writes a record. One button copies it for support.
