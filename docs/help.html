Help

The game crashed

Next launch, open Settings → If something goes wrong. If the last session died, the record is there: what died, where, which switches were on, and the last minute of what Kuro was doing — as codes, no account data. Copy the record to Downloads and send the file. That record is usually the whole diagnosis.

A teleport stopped short / took forever

Read the status line at the top of the Teleport page — it always says which state it's in:

  • transmitting… — the map-transmit leg, with its loading screen. Up to ~10 s on a slow load.
  • X m to go, hop N — paced mode crawling. Check your pace, or switch paced off.
  • arrived / moved to … — done; if you're not where you expected, say where you are vs where you aimed.
  • gave up … short after N hops — beyond the hop budget. Report it with from → to.
  • cancelled — something (you, or a new press) stopped it.

Landed under the map

Should be fixed in 2.0 (the landing is guarded while terrain streams). If it still happens: where from → where to, approximate or exact pin, and whether No Fall Damage was on.

A waypoint won't unlock

Go stand next to it through Teleport Points. If Auto Waypoint presses and it opens — working as designed. If no prompt appears at all, it's quest-sealed: finish the local quest first. No tool opens those.

Nothing presses / no button at range

  • Is the matching Auto switch actually on? Nothing presses with its switch off — by design.
  • Range extensions evaluate while you move. Walk toward the thing from 30+ m and watch where the prompt pops; standing still proves nothing.
  • Wait for the badge after launch. Early presses land before the hooks do.

Menu / overlay issues

Badge missing after load → relaunch once; still missing → send a crash record (there may be a silent death behind it). Sliders jumping → drag, don't tap. Menu open but frozen → close it, wait two seconds, reopen.

FAQ

  • Ban? — see Safety. Short version: possible, behave accordingly, alt first.
  • New game version out, Kuro broken? — expected. Wait for the release that names your version; don't force old builds.
  • iOS / PC / emulator? — Android arm64 only.
  • Controllers? — touch-first; the NoClip stick is on-screen.
  • Can I hide the badge for screenshots? — overlay settings; Hide damage numbers keeps footage clean.
  • Does it work offline / in airplane mode? — no. Online game, online tool.
