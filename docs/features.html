Features

Everything lives behind the floating badge. Quick buttons beside it jump straight to a quest marker, chest, oculus, waypoint, or picked item. Every switch carries a risk pill: local stays on your device, server-checkable could be noticed in data, sent to server is directly visible. Details in Safety.

Player

Protection

  • God mode server-checkable — damage doesn't land.
  • No fall damage local — turn it on before dropping onto approximate pins (see Teleport).

Movement

  • Speed server-checkable — a multiplier on your movement. Keep it sane; absurd values are the oldest ban in the book.
  • NoClip flight server-checkable — gravity off, velocity-driven flight with an on-screen stick. Smooth since 2.0 (the position is now written at the start of the frame).

Combat

  • Enemy targeting and damage controls server-checkable.
  • Hide damage numbers local — display only. Your screenshots stay clean; nothing about the damage changes.

World

ESP

Marks what is loaded around you: a ring with a picture or a symbol, name and distance, with smoothing, fade in/out, off-screen arrows, and an optional outline box. Toggles for names, distance, arrows, boxes, and max distance.

What to mark

People and things by kind — chests, waypoints, statues, puzzles, monsters, NPCs, players, gadgets — each with a live count of what's in sight. Done ones (visited, or activated per the map) are not counted and not offered twice.

Items

Every gatherable, ore, oculus and pickup of this version — 141 of them — by name and picture. Pick one and it is marked in the world and teleportable; nothing else of its kind is shown. Only picked items hides everything else.

Time & weather

Clock and weather controls for screenshots and farming conditions.

Teleport

Go — nearest thing in sight

Chest, waypoint, statue: the nearest new one around you, one press. Landing is slightly above the target, never inside it.

Offline points

Everything the packs and the map know, by region — chests, oculi, specialties from measured world positions, plus newer regions from map pins (flagged approximate: you arrive high and drop, so turn on No Fall Damage).

  • Go to nearest — the closest unfinished point of the selection.
  • Auto farm — the whole selection, nearest first. Waits your delay at each point (500 ms – 5 s slider), collects through Auto Loot / Auto Open / reach, judges, nudges once onto the exact spot if needed, skips if still there. Collected points are remembered on disk, so a second run only visits what's left. Forget collected resets.
  • Teleport Points — every waypoint and statue of the region, positions from the game's own scene data, exact. A locked one is the whole point: you go stand next to it and Auto Waypoint / Auto Statue presses the prompt, like a walk-up. Only activated and already-pressed ones are skipped. If the game offers no prompt at all (quest-sealed), the farm moves on.

Picked items & quest marker

Teleport to the nearest picked item, or track the quest marker and jump to it.

How it moves

Safe mode is on by default: long hauls start with the game's own map transmit (indistinguishable from a map click), then close the remainder in short hops that stay inside the engine's no-packet envelope. After any long jump, gravity holds briefly and the position is guarded while the terrain streams in, so you don't fall through the world. Paced mode (optional) crawls the distance at a set speed instead of jumping. Save point / Recall / Up 5 m included.

Settings

  • Display — FOV, FPS cap.
  • Overlay — badge, quick buttons, menu behavior.
  • Profiles — save and swap whole configurations.
  • Harvest — records what you walk past; export the count as JSON.
  • If something goes wrong — the last crash record, if any, with a button that copies it for support. See Help.
  • Safety — the master switches behind the risk pills.
